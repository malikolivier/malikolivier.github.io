Hello, my name is Mariku. I'm a [technical founder](https://www.stablebuild.com/) interested in developer infrastructure and supply chain security. I also happen to be a [beekeeper](https://kimoto-kinoko.jp/) with a PhD in Computer Science.

I also sometimes go by another name, Malik Olivier Boussejra. Boussejra is the "French"-ified spelling of a Moroccan surname meaning word-for-word "father of the tree" in the local language, hence Kimoto (木本), which means "origin of the tree".
