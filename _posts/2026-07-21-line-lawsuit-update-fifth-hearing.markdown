A short update on my [lawsuit against LINE](/2025/08/14/soft-banned-from-line.html). There was a hearing on July 21, 2026.

LINE argued that the connections in [their third brief](/2026/07/20/line-lawsuit-update-line-third-brief.html) fall under the grounds for suspension in the terms of service, and said they don't plan to file anything more for now.

Now it is my turn to respond. We will file a rebuttal to their third brief and their evidence. The court said that, depending on what we argue there, it may then ask both sides how they want to proceed.

I'll write again once our rebuttal is in.
