A month after [X suspended and quickly restored](/2026/10/09/suspended-by-x-restored-within-an-hour.html) the account of my company, [@StableBuildInc](https://x.com/StableBuildInc), we got locked out again. On November 13, logging in only brought me to a page asking me to confirm I am a human, by scanning a QR code with my phone.

{% include figure.html
    url='/images/x/confirm-human.jpg'
    caption='X asking me to confirm I am a human'
%}

So I scanned it with my phone. Then on my phone, X said it could not confirm that I am a human.

{% include figure.html
    url='/images/x/not-human.jpg'
    caption='"We could not confirm that you are a human."'
%}

We appealed to X Support, but unlike last time, the account is still locked a week later. So for now, we can't log into our official account.

Last time, I wrote that I did not expect X's support to be more helpful than LINE's, but maybe I spoke too soon.

Interestingly, this week, we were also locked out of Reddit, after appealing, we were back within a day.
It seems that with all the bots around, it's now very hard to run a legitimate business on social networks.
