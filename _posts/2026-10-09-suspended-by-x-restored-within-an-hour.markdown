Today, the X account of my company, [@StableBuildInc](https://x.com/StableBuildInc), was suspended. For context, it's a free account: we don't pay X anything.
The email only said the account had been reported for breaking X's rules against "inauthentic behaviors", without saying what we did wrong.

{% include figure.html
    url='/images/x/suspended.png'
    caption='The suspension notice from X'
%}

Expecting the worst (i.e. no answer from support), we sent an appeal through X's public form. Less than an hour later, X Support wrote back that there had been no violation and that the account was restored to full functionality!

{% include figure.html
    url='/images/x/restored.png'
    caption='X Support restoring the account after the appeal'
%}

Compare that with [LINE](/2025/08/14/soft-banned-from-line.html), where my account has been suspended for about a year and a half and it took a lawsuit and more than a year just to learn what they had detected. I didn't expect X's support to turn out more helpful than LINE's.
